FLING brand

Slogan: No custody. No fee. One signature. Tagline: Fling it. FLING is a memecoin launchpad. The mark is the flick: one tapered arc and a dot thrown off its end. The wordmark sets FLING in a heavy grotesque with a dotless i; the dot is flung up and to the right so the word carries the mark's gesture. Two shapes, one colour. Everything here is vector and single-colour by design, so it survives a favicon, an embroidery, a monochrome filing and a billboard without change.

The mark

Ink on paper, paper on night, paper on Fling Orange, Fling Orange on paper. Those are the only four treatments.

The wordmark

Set from outlines, never from a live font. The dot is part of the drawing, not a glyph.

Mark: never below 16 px. Lockup: never below 110 px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Clear space

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the flung dot's throw (the distance from the top of the N to the dot). Nothing else enters that zone.

Colour

Ink#111111type and mark on paper
Paper#FFFFFFlight ground
Night#120F0Ddark ground

No gradients, no second accent, no outlines, no shadows on the mark.

Do not

  • Rotate, skew or mirror the flick.
  • Change the taper, the gap, or the dot's size or position.
  • Add a second colour inside the mark or wordmark.
  • Set the wordmark from a font; use the outlines.
  • Put the dot back on the i.
  • Place the lockup on busy imagery or low-contrast colour.
  • Use the pixel slingshot from earlier builds. It is retired.
  • Stretch to fit; scale proportionally.
