FLING brand

Slogan: No custody. No fee. One signature. Tagline: Fling it. FLING is a memecoin launchpad. The mark is the skip: a stone flung off the water. One wave, one launch trail, one stone. The wordmark sets FLING in a heavy grotesque with a dotless i; the dot is the stone, thrown up and to the right so the word carries the mark's gesture. Three shapes, one colour. Everything here is vector and single-colour by design, so it survives a favicon, an embroidery, a monochrome filing and a billboard without change.

The mark

Ink on paper, paper on deep, paper on Fling Blue, Fling Blue on paper. Those are the only four treatments.

Anatomy

The waterOne wave, a stroke and a half long, round ends. It is the only element that touches the ground of the mark.
The launchA trail that starts as a hair at the crest and thickens toward the stone. It leaves the water at the first bounce and never comes back.
The stoneA solid disc in the top right, the heaviest shape in the mark. In the wordmark it is the dot of the i, flung.

The wordmark

Set from outlines, never from a live font. The dot is part of the drawing, not a glyph.

Mark: never below 16 px. Lockup: never below 110 px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Clear space

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the stone's diameter. Nothing else enters that zone.

Colour

Fling Blue#1D5FFFthe one brand colour
Fling Blue, on deep#3B82FFthe same blue lifted for dark screens
Ink#0E1526type and mark on paper
Paper#FFFFFFlight ground
Deep#0A0E17dark ground

No gradients, no second accent, no outlines, no shadows on the mark. On screens the blue lifts one step on the dark ground so it keeps its contrast; in print it is always #1D5FFF.

$Fling token

The token art lives in one file, fling-token.svg, and is shown on the token section of the launch page. Until the final token art is supplied it is the mark in Fling Blue.

Do not

  • Rotate, skew or mirror the skip.
  • Change the wave count, the trail taper, or the stone's size or position.
  • Add a second colour inside the mark or wordmark.
  • Set the wordmark from a font; use the outlines.
  • Put the dot back on the i.
  • Place the lockup on busy imagery or low-contrast colour.
  • Use the orange flick or the pixel slingshot from earlier builds. Both are retired.
  • Stretch to fit; scale proportionally.
