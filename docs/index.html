FLING is a non-custodial launchpad for memecoins on Solana. One form, one wallet signature, and your coin is live on pump.fun. These pages explain exactly what happens, what it costs, what the risks are, and what the rules are.

In one paragraph

You connect a Solana wallet. You type a name and a ticker, drop in the art, and optionally add links and an initial buy. The page shrinks the art, stores it and a metadata file permanently on Arweave through Irys (you sign one message; small files are free), generates a fresh mint keypair in your browser, asks PumpPortal's public transaction builder for a pump.fun create transaction, signs it with the mint key and your wallet, and sends it to Solana. The bonding curve opens the moment the transaction confirms. FLING never holds your keys, your SOL or your tokens, and charges no fee of its own.
