How it works

  1. 01Fill the form

    Name, ticker, description, art. Links are optional. The preview on the right is exactly what the coin page will show.

  2. 02Art gets pinned

    Your image and a metadata file are pinned to IPFS. The coin points at that file forever, so nothing here can change it later.

  3. 03One signature

    A pump.fun create transaction is built with a fresh mint keypair generated in your browser. Your wallet shows it; you approve it; it is sent.

  4. 04It is live

    The bonding curve opens immediately. You get the mint address, the pump.fun page and the explorer link. Trade it, share it, or fling the next one.

FLING never holds your keys or your funds. Transactions are built by the public PumpPortal API and signed by you. Memecoins are volatile; launch and trade at your own risk. Nothing here is financial advice.
